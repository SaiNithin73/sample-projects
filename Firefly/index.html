No. I ✦
This very evening, by candlelight  ♡
♡
My Dearest,
There are a thousand things I wish I could say —
in a thousand different ways — and yet
none of them would ever be enough.

You walk into a room and the whole world
quietly rearranges itself around you.

I wanted you to know that you are thought of —
not occasionally, not in passing — but with the
kind of warmth that lingers long after
the candle has gone out.

You are, without question, someone worth
writing letters to.
Always & completely yours, ♡
P.S. — You deserved to read something beautiful today.
